# Jewel bundles

At Lahap (including the Illusion Temple bundling NPC), combine jewels into bundles of
10, 20 or 30. All configured jewel mixes support loose jewels and whole smaller
bundles of the same jewel type: two bundles of 10 make 20; three bundles of 10,
a bundle of 20 plus 10, or smaller bundles plus loose jewels can make 30.

Materials must exactly fill the requested quantity. The service never splits a
larger bundle or returns change. A bundle already matching the requested size is
left alone. Extra materials remain in the inventory. Larger eligible bundles are
used first, then inventory slot order, then loose jewels.

The existing combine fee remains 500,000 zen per ten output jewels. Insufficient
materials or money leave the inventory and money unchanged. The updated MuMain
client is required for its local material check to recognize bundles; the original
client may still require loose jewels. Deploy the matching server update first.
